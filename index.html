Privacy Policy

How your learning data and privacy are handled

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Data Collection

Subnetting Trainer does not collect, store on external servers, or transmit directly identifying personal information such as your name, email address, or precise location.

Learning Progress

Learning progress, exercise results, and app settings may be stored locally on your device to provide training features. This information is not sent to the developer.

Diagnostics and Analytics

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Purchases

If premium features are offered, purchases are processed securely through Apple StoreKit and the App Store. The app does not directly process or store your payment details.

Third-Party Services

Apple may process information related to App Store downloads and in-app purchases according to its own privacy policy.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or share personal information. App Store transactions are handled by Apple.
